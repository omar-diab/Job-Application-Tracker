You apply to 20, 50, 100 jobs. Links are lost in tabs, statuses live in your head, and you forget who you should follow up with.

**Job Application Tracker keeps it all in one place, automatically.**

## 🌐 Works where you search

**LinkedIn** · **Indeed** (every country) · **company career pages** · and almost any job site.
If a site can't be read, you can still save the job and fill in the details yourself.

## 🔒 Your data stays yours

- Your jobs are saved **only on your computer**. Never on a server, never shared.
- **No account**, no sign-up, no tracking.
- The extension reads a page **only when you click its icon**.

[Read the privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)

## 💬 FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free?</b></summary>

Yes. Job Application Tracker is free. It shows one small card marked "Sponsored" inside the extension — never on the websites you visit.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can you see my job applications?</b></summary>

No. Everything is saved in your own browser, on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which websites does it work with?</b></summary>

LinkedIn, Indeed and most company career pages. On any other page, the page title is used and you can edit the details before saving.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I move my list to Excel?</b></summary>

Yes. Click <b>Export CSV</b> in the dashboard. The file opens correctly in Excel and Google Sheets, including Arabic text.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which languages are available?</b></summary>

English, English (UK), العربية, Deutsch, Español, Français, Bahasa Indonesia, Português (Brasil), Русский, Türkçe, हिन्दी, 简体中文 and 日本語.
</details>
